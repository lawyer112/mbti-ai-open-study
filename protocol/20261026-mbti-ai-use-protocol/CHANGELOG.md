# 修订记录 / Changelog

## 0.4.0 · 2026-10-27

按发起人反馈改为 MBTI × AI 趣味调查：日常使用、聊天习惯、好感与吐槽。问卷更新为 v0.3，选择题共 19 项、故事版 11 项，共享 7 项，23 个不同题项；近期非使用者和不确定类型均可参加。取消旧 C14 主要指标及单任务回顾要求。完整提供 12 语言的问卷、题目目的、回传表单、介绍、设计说明和同源 PDF。保留语言/版本及逐字引用许可，不把旧 v0.2 任务报告与新习惯报告混算。没有真人统计结果。

Reframed as an informal MBTI × AI survey of everyday uses, conversations, praise and complaints. Instrument v0.3 has 19 choice items, 11 story items, 7 shared and 23 distinct, including recent nonusers and uncertain types. Retired the C14 primary-outcome plan and single-task recall requirement. Added 12 complete localized questionnaires, purposes, return forms, introductions, guides and matching PDFs. Preserve language/version and quotation permissions; keep old v0.2 task records separate. No human statistical findings.

## 0.3.0 · 2026-10-26

首次公开方案包：补齐完整中英全文、共享参考文献、论断证据、术语表、同源网页/PDF和核验记录。增加研究问题与题目映射，明确正式阶段主要探索指标为外部核验，区分行为关联与客观绩效研究。问卷题意维持中文v0.2；导出补充中文语言和试填阶段标记，并接入真实回传入口。无真人结果发表。

First public protocol package: complete Chinese and English texts, shared references, an evidence record, glossary, HTML/PDF from the same sources, and verification records. Added the research-question/item mapping, specified external verification as the intended primary exploratory outcome, and separated behavioral associations from objective performance research. Instrument wording remains Chinese v0.2; exports add language and pilot-stage metadata and use the actual return pathway. No human-participant findings are published.
