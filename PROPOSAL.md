# 从一次趣味调查开始 / Start with an informal survey

这是一个 MBTI × AI 趣味调查。我们想收集真实的日常习惯、评价和小故事，看看不同人格标签的人拿 AI 做什么、怎样和它交流、喜欢什么又吐槽什么。既看类型之间有没有倾向，也看同一类型的人能有多不一样。没有预设哪种人格更厉害，你也不需要证明自己会用 AI。

有了真实回答以后，我们希望逐步做出《16 种人格与 AI 相处图鉴》：使用习惯图表、好感与吐槽分布、获得引用许可的真实小故事，以及持续更新的观察文章。每次都说明有效人数和各类型样本数；没有数据的类型留空。差异很小、例外很多，也是值得分享的发现。这些是计划，当前没有参与者统计结果。

This is an informal MBTI × AI survey. We want real everyday habits, opinions and stories: what people use AI for, how they talk to it, what they enjoy and what annoys them. We will look for tendencies between reported types and variety within each type. There is no predetermined winning personality, and you do not need to prove your AI skills.

Once real responses arrive, we hope to build an atlas of how 16 personality types live with AI: habit charts, praise and complaint distributions, true stories quoted with permission, and an evolving observation article. We will state usable response counts and counts for each type; types without data stay blank. Small differences and many exceptions are interesting too. These are planned outputs; no participant statistics are currently reported.
