## English equivalent

Complete the same report fields in English if preferred:

1. **Question:** which AI-use behaviors or outcomes did the study examine?
2. **Sample and recruitment:** dates, region/language, recruitment, inclusion/exclusion, distinct people and record counts, type distribution and missingness, and overlap with other submissions or studies.
3. **Measurement and process:** personality instrument/version/date or self-reported labels; survey/interview questions; tasks and AI conditions; implementation, controls or randomization (state none if absent); outcomes, coding and rating methods.
4. **Analysis and findings:** methods, exclusions, results including null findings, supported and unsupported conclusions, and links to data, code, materials and the report.
5. **Rights and participation:** authorship and authority to share, participant awareness and permitted research/public uses, removal of identifying information or aggregate-only scope, ethics review or exemption when applicable, and AI assistance in writing, coding or analysis.

A study report is not automatically an individual observation or part of a pooled model. Outcomes, tasks, permissions and sample overlap must be checked first.
